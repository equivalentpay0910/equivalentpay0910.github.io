About

I'm a Data Science student at San José State University (minors in Statistics + Computer Science, graduating Dec 2027). I like building things people actually use, and I care most about making AI systems you can trust.

I'm also the team's de facto PM, setting the roadmap, running weekly sprints and keeping product, design + engineering in sync.

Recent builds include a full-stack agent on Anthropic's Claude tool-calling API that decides on its own which tools to call and when, and a multimodal Trust & Safety risk pipeline. Through a Stanford SALT Lab-affiliated program, I'm also hardening a tool-using agent and a RAG system against adversarial attacks.

Before all this, I was a Deep Learning Intern at Hewlett Packard Enterprise in Singapore, building ANN, RNN and GAN models, training a posture-detection model to 93% accuracy and learning what it takes to move a model from a notebook to a deployment pipeline.
